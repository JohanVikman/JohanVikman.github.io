It's time to find something new to do on the internet. I used to have a physical news paper, which I read in the morning with a cup of coffee. Then I was off news until the next morning, and some days I just skimmed the content and went straight for the crosswords.... So if something "news-worth" happened, no one could sell it to me, I was not available.

Compare that to today, I've got a couple of news apps on my phone, I got social media, there's news on TV, emails, and more. It's impossible to keep up and frankly I don't need most of the news.

But I do it out of habit. A habit I want to get rid off, and getting rid of habits can be hard. Instead you replace the bad habit with a good one. Enter blogging.

I hope blogging can give me a creative way to spend my time. I almost wrote productive, but I don't think I should settle for fun. If it isn't fun I'll quit, if it's productive I might feel I have to continue even thouhg it isn't fun.

So just fun. Not productive.
